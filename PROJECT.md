## Vision

Unofficial native HDrezka client for macOS 15+ and iPadOS 18+ (with tvOS work). Users get browsing, search, bookmarks, account auth, comments, a custom video player, and downloads without relying on a browser. Success means reliable native playback and account features across Apple platforms.
